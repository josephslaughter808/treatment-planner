## Current Recommendation

ClearPath is currently a pre-seed, pre-revenue, pre-pilot health-technology company with a working prototype and substantial research and grant planning, but without validated clinical traction or production readiness for protected health information.

The preferred sequence is:

1. Complete an investor-readiness sprint and customer discovery.
2. Continue the NLM/NIMHD/NSF grant process.
3. Seek a small number of healthcare angels and accelerators for feedback and introductions.
4. If capital is needed before a grant decision, consider a $500,000 to $750,000 post-money SAFE round.
5. Use a working valuation-cap range of $5 million to $8 million, with approximately $7 million as a defensible initial discussion point after the readiness work is complete.
6. Avoid a priced equity round until ClearPath has stronger team, pilot, customer, and security evidence.

This is planning analysis, not legal, tax, accounting, investment, or valuation advice. Securities counsel and a qualified tax adviser should review any offering before ClearPath discusses binding terms or accepts money.
