# Valuation And Dilution Model

## Important Distinction

An early-stage company does not have one objectively correct valuation. A SAFE valuation cap is a negotiated conversion term, not an appraisal, audited fair-market value, tax valuation, or guaranteed company value.

ClearPath's current fundraising terms should reflect what the market will accept while preserving enough founder ownership for future hiring and financing.

## Current Stage Assessment

Positive factors:

- Working prototype and active codebase
- Large, expensive healthcare interoperability problem
- Patient-control and cross-office workflow thesis
- Substantial grant, research, and architecture preparation
- Multiple non-dilutive funding possibilities
- Founder commitment and domain problem insight

Discount factors:

- Pre-revenue
- No completed pilot or signed customer
- No demonstrated product-market fit
- No production PHI readiness
- No independent security validation
- No established full-time technical leader
- Long enterprise healthcare sales and integration cycles
- Significant legal, privacy, security, and interoperability execution risk
- Investor-grade corporate and IP readiness not yet confirmed

## Market Reference

Carta's 2025 pre-seed data reported median post-money SAFE caps around:

- $10 million for rounds between $250,000 and $1 million
- $15 million for rounds between $1 million and $2.5 million

Those are medians across companies on Carta, not automatic entitlements. ClearPath currently lacks several traction signals expected of companies receiving top-half terms.

## Recommended Working Range

### Current Negotiation Range

- Conservative: $5 million post-money cap
- Preferred discussion point: $7 million post-money cap
- Strong outcome: $8 million post-money cap
- Not currently recommended without more evidence: $10 million or higher

This range should be revisited after customer interviews, pilot letters, technical recruitment, corporate cleanup, and grant feedback.

## SAFE Dilution Table

Approximate ownership represented by a post-money SAFE is investment divided by post-money cap, before dilution from the later priced round and option-pool changes.

| Raise | $5M cap | $6M cap | $7M cap | $8M cap | $10M cap |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $500,000 | 10.00% | 8.33% | 7.14% | 6.25% | 5.00% |
| $750,000 | 15.00% | 12.50% | 10.71% | 9.38% | 7.50% |
| $1,000,000 | 20.00% | 16.67% | 14.29% | 12.50% | 10.00% |

The preferred planning case is $750,000 at a $7 million post-money cap, representing approximately 10.7% before later dilution.

## Why Not Maximize The Cap

A very high cap reduces immediate dilution but can:

- Make the round harder to close
- Signal unrealistic expectations
- Create a difficult next-round valuation
- Reduce investor alignment
- Encourage unfavorable side terms
- Lead to a down round if milestones are missed

A lower cap closes more easily but can sell too much ownership before value-creating milestones. The goal is a credible price for a milestone-funded round, not the largest number in the deck.

## Milestone-Based Reassessment

These are planning bands, not forecasts:

| Evidence level | Possible discussion range | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current prototype, no pilots | $5M-$8M SAFE cap | Present stage |
| Technical lead, clean company/IP, 2-3 strong pilot letters | $7M-$10M SAFE cap | Execution and commercial risk reduced |
| Completed synthetic pilot, security review, grant award or strong customer commitments | $8M-$12M SAFE cap | Feasibility and financing risk reduced |
| Live compliant pilot and early recurring revenue | Negotiate a priced seed round from current traction | Revenue, growth, retention, pipeline, and margins become more important than this table |

Milestones do not mechanically add dollars to valuation. Investor demand, market conditions, terms, founder leverage, competition, and quality of evidence still control.

## Option Pool And Future Dilution

ClearPath may need equity to recruit a technical cofounder, technical lead, independent directors, or key employees. A future investor may require an option pool to be created or increased before their investment, which often dilutes existing holders.

Before issuing a SAFE, model:

- Founder shares
- Any cofounder or advisor grants
- Existing promises of equity
- SAFE ownership
- A possible 10%-15% employee option pool
- Seed-round dilution
- Pro-rata rights

Do not promise percentage ownership by email or handshake. Every grant should be board-approved, documented, vested where appropriate, and reflected in the cap table.

## Example Future Dilution

Illustrative only:

1. A $750,000 post-money SAFE at a $7 million cap represents about 10.7%.
2. A later option-pool increase and priced seed round could dilute both founder and SAFE holders.
3. A 20% priced seed round would reduce the founder's then-current percentage by approximately one-fifth, before considering pool mechanics.

This is why the company must model the entire financing path rather than looking only at the first SAFE.

## Valuation Evidence File

Maintain a file supporting the fundraising position:

- Product demonstration and technical architecture
- Customer discovery summaries
- Pilot letters and pipeline
- Market and competition analysis
- Security and regulatory plan
- Grant status
- Team biographies and commitments
- 18-month milestones and budget
- Comparable financings where reliable data is available
- Cap-table and dilution model

## Recommendation

Use $7 million as an internal planning cap, not a public declaration that ClearPath is “worth $7 million.” Be prepared to close within a $5 million to $8 million range if the investor quality, check size, rights, and company runway justify it. Reassess after the investor-readiness sprint.
