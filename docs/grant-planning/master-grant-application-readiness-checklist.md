# ClearPath Master Grant Application Readiness Checklist

## Purpose

This is the working master checklist for preparing ClearPath's first federal small-business grant application. It combines the funding-path, research, product, compliance, staffing, budget, pilot, and commercialization work already documented in this folder.

Use it as a living control document. Every checked item should have supporting evidence saved in the application workspace. Requirements vary by agency and Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO), so the selected NOFO and agency instructions always control.

Priority labels used below:

- **BLOCKER** - must be complete before submission.
- **STRENGTHENER** - not always administratively required, but materially improves competitiveness.
- **CONDITIONAL** - complete if triggered by the selected mechanism, research design, or agency.
- **DEFER** - document the plan now; perform the full work after award or in Phase II.

## Gate 1: Funding Path And Opportunity Selection

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Freeze the grant-facing product definition.** Use one discipline-neutral description: ClearPath is a patient-controlled, interoffice and interdisciplinary health-information request and exchange platform.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Freeze the Phase I research objective.** State the technical and human-factors uncertainty that Phase I will resolve, not merely the software features to be built.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm the primary funding path.** Compare NLM, NIBIB, AHRQ, NSF Seed Fund, and conditional alternatives against product fit, mechanism, budget, timeline, and eligibility.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Select the strongest agency, institute, or center.** Record why the chosen home fits better than the alternatives and what product distortion it does or does not require.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Identify the exact active NOFO or solicitation.** Save the official announcement, amendments, related notices, and the agency's current application guide.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm the NOFO is open on the intended submission date.** Verify opening date, expiration date, standard or special due dates, and local submission time.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm the accepted mechanism.** Determine whether ClearPath will use SBIR Phase I, STTR Phase I, Fast-Track, Direct-to-Phase-II, research grant, contract solicitation, or another path.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm whether clinical trials are allowed, required, optional, or prohibited.** Match the answer to the proposed research design.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm participating organizations.** Verify that the chosen institute or center participates in the exact NOFO and accepts applications in ClearPath's topic area.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm award ceiling and project period.** Record direct-cost limits, total-cost limits, statutory guidelines, waiver rules, and the maximum Phase I duration.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm submission rules.** Record whether the application uses ASSIST, Grants.gov Workspace, Research.gov, a contract portal, or another system.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm resubmission, duplicate-submission, and overlapping-application rules.** Prevent conflicting applications or prohibited simultaneous submissions.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Create a one-page opportunity decision memo.** Include fit, mechanism, amount, duration, deadline, eligibility, major risks, and recommendation.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Preserve a ranked backup list.** Record the next two viable opportunities and the changes each would require.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Build a compliance matrix from the NOFO.** Map every instruction, attachment, page limit, and review criterion to an owner and evidence file.

## Gate 2: Program Officer And Grant Manager Engagement

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Identify the correct program contact.** Confirm the program officer, scientific contact, small-business contact, grants-management contact, and technical submission help desk.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Prepare a one-page Specific Aims concept summary.** Include problem, gap, innovation, aims, methods, expected outcomes, company, and commercialization relevance.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Prepare a concise product description.** Explain ClearPath without dental framing, excessive feature detail, or unsupported claims.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Prepare a focused question list.** Cover mission fit, mechanism, Phase I scope, PI eligibility, budget, human-subjects expectations, synthetic data, and Phase II transition.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Contact the program officer before submission.** Send the concept summary and request a short fit discussion when allowed.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document the conversation.** Record date, attendees, guidance, cautions, recommended mechanism, and follow-up actions.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Resolve PI-employment questions.** Confirm whether the founder may serve as PI and the required primary-employment level during the award.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Resolve budget questions.** Confirm founder salary, technical-lead labor, consultants, participant costs, cloud costs, legal review, security review, and indirect costs.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Resolve Phase I security scope.** Confirm whether synthetic or de-identified data plus architecture review is acceptable and whether penetration testing can wait until Phase II.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Resolve research-partner expectations.** Ask whether a university, health-services researcher, clinical partner, or subcontract materially improves fit or is required.
- [ ] **CONDITIONAL - Request written clarification for ambiguous instructions.** Save the response in the application record.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Ask the program officer to react to the aims, not edit the proposal.** Use feedback to correct fit and scope before drafting is locked.

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm no disqualifying foreign ownership or control.** Complete any required foreign-risk disclosure accurately.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Obtain or verify the EIN.** Ensure the IRS legal name and address match registration records.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Verify active business good standing.** Resolve franchise-tax, registered-agent, assumed-name, or annual-report issues.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Establish a business bank account.** Ensure award payments and project expenses can be segregated and reconciled.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Establish basic accounting controls.** Use a chart of accounts capable of separating direct labor, fringe, consultants, subawards, other direct costs, and indirect costs.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Establish timekeeping.** Create contemporaneous labor tracking for the founder, employees, and any personnel charged to the award.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document founder authority.** Record who may sign applications, certifications, contracts, and award documents.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm intellectual-property ownership.** Ensure the company owns or has written rights to the code, designs, documentation, domain, data model, and contractor work product.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Inventory open-source and third-party licenses.** Flag copyleft, attribution, commercial-use, model-asset, dataset, and hosted-service restrictions.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Put contractor IP and confidentiality agreements in place.** Do this before relying on outside-developed material in the application.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Create a capitalization table.** Keep it current and consistent with registrations and investor materials.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Create a conflict-of-interest disclosure process.** Capture financial interests, related parties, and organizational conflicts.
- [ ] **CONDITIONAL - Obtain required insurance.** Determine when general liability, cyber, professional liability, workers' compensation, or directors-and-officers coverage becomes necessary.

## Gate 5: Research Question, Specific Aims, And Scientific Strategy

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the central research question.** Frame a feasibility question about consent-scoped exchange, authorization fidelity, provenance, usability, workflow burden, or interoperability.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the knowledge gap.** Explain what is not known and why routine software development cannot answer it without structured research.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - State the overall hypothesis or feasibility proposition.** Make it testable within the Phase I period.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Limit the project to two or three coherent Specific Aims.** Each aim must be measurable, feasible, and necessary to the overall conclusion.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define Aim 1 deliverables and success criteria.** Include required data, method, analysis, risks, and alternative approach.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define Aim 2 deliverables and success criteria.** Include required data, method, analysis, risks, and alternative approach.
- [ ] **CONDITIONAL - Define Aim 3 only if it can be completed credibly.** Avoid an over-scoped application.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Link every aim to a Phase I milestone.** Align the research strategy, statement of work, timeline, and budget.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define primary and secondary outcomes.** Use operational definitions and measurement instruments.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Establish quantitative success thresholds.** Examples include authorization fidelity, schema-validation rate, task-completion rate, comprehension, usability, time, and error rate.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define failure criteria.** State what result would show the approach is not feasible or needs redesign.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the analysis plan.** Include sample size rationale, descriptive or inferential methods, missing-data handling, and decision rules.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the comparison condition or baseline.** Use current record-request workflows, standard forms, or an appropriate prototype baseline.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the study population and roles.** Distinguish patients, caregivers, clinicians, records staff, office staff, and technical evaluators.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define recruitment and inclusion criteria.** Ensure the population matches the claims and pilot use case.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define data sources.** Identify synthetic records, de-identified datasets, interviews, usability sessions, logs, surveys, and expert review.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the mixed-methods approach.** Explain how quantitative performance data and qualitative workflow feedback will be integrated.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define risks and alternative strategies for each aim.** Include recruitment, comprehension, integration, schema, security, and partner risks.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Explain rigor and reproducibility.** Address protocol consistency, version control, scripted tests, preregistered decision rules where useful, and transparent reporting.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Obtain an experienced research-methods review.** Use a health-services, biomedical-informatics, human-factors, or implementation-science researcher.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Create a one-page logic model.** Connect inputs, activities, outputs, short-term outcomes, and Phase II impact.

## Gate 6: Significance, Innovation, And Product Narrative

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Quantify the problem without exaggeration.** Support fragmentation, administrative burden, incomplete records, and patient-messenger claims with credible sources.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the affected users and settings.** Identify the first interdisciplinary workflows rather than claiming all healthcare at once.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Explain why current approaches are insufficient.** Compare portals, EHR exchange, HIEs, fax, release-of-information services, secure messaging, and manual patient transport.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the scientific significance.** Connect authorization fidelity, provenance, comprehension, and workflow outcomes to safer information exchange.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the technical innovation.** Describe the canonical ClearPath JSON model, deterministic consent scope, provenance, validation, translator contract, and auditability.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the human-centered innovation.** Explain patient-readable requests, section-level choices, attribution, corrections, and revocation.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Separate innovation from ordinary product features.** Do not present login, dashboards, uploads, or generic APIs as the research contribution.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Explain why the approach is interdisciplinary.** Use examples spanning primary care, specialty care, hospital discharge, rehabilitation, behavioral health, pharmacy, laboratory, and imaging.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Remove legacy dental framing.** Audit narrative, screenshots, examples, terminology, diagrams, and file names used in the submission.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Avoid unsupported clinical claims.** ClearPath does not diagnose, prescribe, replace an EHR, guarantee HIPAA compliance, or guarantee improved outcomes.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Build a concise competitive matrix.** Compare patient control, consent granularity, provenance, interoperability, small-practice accessibility, and implementation burden.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Create one consistent elevator pitch.** Use it across the abstract, narrative, commercialization plan, letters, and presentation.

## Gate 7: Technical Feasibility Evidence And Prototype Readiness

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Freeze the Phase I minimum viable research prototype.** Exclude features that do not test the aims.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Demonstrate the provider record-request flow.** Include patient selection, requested sections, purpose, recipient, expiration, and status.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Demonstrate the patient decision flow.** Include approve all, approve selected sections, deny, request clarification, and revoke where in scope.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Demonstrate deterministic consent enforcement.** Prove denied sections cannot enter the generated package.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Demonstrate ClearPath JSON generation.** Use a versioned schema, validation, canonical field names, and controlled examples.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Demonstrate provider review.** Show human-readable sections, source attribution, status, and download or acknowledgement behavior.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Demonstrate audit events.** Cover request creation, delivery, decision, package generation, view, download, expiration, denial, and revocation.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Create representative synthetic patient records.** Include common, sensitive, conflicting, missing, historical, and multi-source data.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Create negative test cases.** Test malformed packages, missing consent, expired consent, wrong recipient, duplicate events, and unauthorized sections.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Run schema and package smoke tests.** Save reproducible test results and known limitations.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define provenance behavior.** Every clinical item should preserve source, author or organization, timestamp, import method, and verification state when available.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define correction and disagreement behavior.** Distinguish patient-entered, provider-entered, imported, disputed, corrected, and superseded information.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document the reviewed-import-first architecture.** Direct EHR writes are not a Phase I dependency.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document input and output priorities.** Keep Phase I centered on ClearPath JSON, human-readable review, PDF, and CSV unless the NOFO requires more.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Produce an architecture diagram.** Show identity, request, consent, package, translation, storage, audit, and provider-review boundaries.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document technical uncertainties.** Identify what must be researched rather than implying the full solution already exists.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Record a polished prototype demonstration.** Use synthetic data and a scripted interdisciplinary scenario.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Create a prototype evidence appendix.** Include screenshots, test summaries, schema excerpts, and workflow maps as permitted.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Establish baseline performance measurements.** Record current package generation, validation, loading, and workflow timing.
- [ ] **DEFER - Plan direct FHIR, HL7 v2, C-CDA, DICOM, NCPDP, and proprietary integrations for later phases.** Do not promise unsupported production integrations in Phase I.

## Gate 8: Privacy, Security, Consent, And Regulatory Planning

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Obtain a scoped healthcare privacy legal review.** Cover HIPAA roles, authorization, state law, sensitive categories, minors, guardians, revocation, and data-sharing assumptions.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Determine ClearPath's likely HIPAA role in each workflow.** Document whether the company may act as a business associate, subcontractor, personal health record vendor, or another role.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Determine whether the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule may apply.** Document the conclusion and triggers.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Identify state-law issues relevant to the planned pilot locations.** Include Texas and any partner state.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Identify specially protected information.** Address behavioral health, substance-use records, reproductive health, HIV/STI, genetic information, and adolescent confidentiality as applicable.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define consent and authorization objects.** Include requester, recipient, purpose, sections, dates, expiration, revocation, and proof of patient decision.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define identity and authority checks.** Cover patient, proxy, caregiver, guardian, dependent, provider, and organization authority.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define minimum-necessary behavior.** Explain how request scope and package generation limit disclosure.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define data retention and deletion assumptions.** Separate research records, application records, prototype data, logs, and future production PHI.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define breach and incident responsibilities at the planning level.** Identify notification, escalation, evidence preservation, and partner coordination.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Complete a Phase I threat model.** Include authentication, authorization, tenant isolation, object access, file access, secrets, logs, exports, and third-party services.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Complete a security architecture review.** Produce prioritized remediation items and an owner for each.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document encryption.** Cover data in transit, data at rest, field-level encryption where used, key management, rotation, and backup protection.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document access controls.** Cover least privilege, role separation, provider verification, administrative access, and service accounts.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document audit logging and monitoring.** Identify events, retention, integrity, alerting, and access review.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Document secure development practices.** Include code review, dependency scanning, secret scanning, environment separation, and vulnerability remediation.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Ensure synthetic or properly de-identified data is used before authorization to handle PHI.** Verify demonstrations, screenshots, logs, and test files.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Prepare draft Business Associate Agreement and data-use concepts if partner conversations require them.** Legal counsel should review before use.
- [ ] **CONDITIONAL - Determine whether the software could be regulated as a medical device.** Document why ClearPath is or is not providing regulated clinical decision support.
- [ ] **DEFER - Schedule full third-party penetration testing before a real-PHI production pilot.** Include budget and acceptance criteria in Phase II.
- [ ] **DEFER - Plan SOC 2, HITRUST, or equivalent assurance based on customer requirements.** Do not claim certification before completion.

## Gate 9: Human Subjects, Ethics, And Data Management

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Determine whether each activity is human-subjects research.** Separate discovery interviews, usability testing, pilot evaluation, product analytics, and clinical research.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Determine whether the project is exempt, non-exempt, not human-subjects research, or requires another determination.** Obtain formal review when required.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Identify the reviewing IRB.** Confirm whether the small business, research partner, or independent IRB will be responsible.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Draft the human-subjects section required by the mechanism.** Address risk, protections, recruitment, consent, privacy, and confidentiality.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define participant inclusion and exclusion criteria.** Ensure they match the aims and proposed claims.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Address inclusion across sex/gender, race/ethnicity, age, disability, language, and digital access where required.** Provide scientific justification for exclusions.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Create a recruitment plan.** Avoid coercion and clarify relationships with pilot offices.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Create participant consent materials.** Use readable language and distinguish research consent from health-information authorization.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define participant compensation.** Confirm allowability, amount, timing, and budget treatment.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Create a privacy-preserving data-collection plan.** Collect only what is needed for the aims.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Create the Data Management and Sharing Plan when required.** Address data types, standards, metadata, access, preservation, oversight, and limitations.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define research-data security.** Cover identifiers, coding, linkage files, storage, access, transfer, retention, and destruction.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define adverse-event or unanticipated-problem reporting if applicable.** Assign responsibility and timelines.
- [ ] **CONDITIONAL - Complete clinical-trial forms and registration planning if the study meets the NIH clinical-trial definition.** Do not guess.
- [ ] **CONDITIONAL - Address a single IRB, reliance agreements, certificates of confidentiality, or data-use agreements.** Complete when triggered.
- [ ] **CONDITIONAL - Address vulnerable populations.** Include children, decisionally impaired adults, prisoners, or economically vulnerable participants only with appropriate protections.
- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Conduct a health-literacy and accessibility review of participant materials.** Include screen-reader, contrast, mobile, language, and cognitive-load considerations.

- [ ] **STRENGTHENER - Obtain customer-discovery evidence.** Document interviews with patients, providers, records staff, office staff, and buyers.

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Confirm the project period and budget periods.** Match months, effort, milestones, and cost timing.

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Validate founder/PI compensation.** Support reasonableness with role, effort, salary basis, company policy, and agency limitations.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Validate technical-lead cost.** Support salary or contractor rate with scope, market basis, effort, and deliverables.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Calculate fringe benefits consistently.** Document included benefits and the rate basis.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Determine the indirect-cost approach.** Use a negotiated rate, de minimis rate, agency-allowed default, or justified provisional method as applicable.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Prevent double counting.** Ensure costs are not charged both directly and indirectly.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Price consultants and subawards.** Obtain quotes, scopes, rate documentation, and required institutional budgets.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Price legal and privacy review.** Tie the amount to Phase I deliverables rather than broad corporate legal work.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Price security architecture review.** Distinguish it from deferred production penetration testing.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Price clinical, interoperability, and research advisors.** Match effort and deliverables.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Price participant and pilot expenses.** Confirm incentives, travel, site costs, and recruitment costs are allowable.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Price cloud and development infrastructure.** Separate prototype needs from normal company overhead where required.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Price software, data, and model licenses.** Include only items necessary for the proposed research.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Address equipment rules.** Justify any item that meets the agency's equipment threshold and avoid unnecessary purchases.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Address travel.** Tie each trip to a research or required grant purpose.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Address publication, data-sharing, and dissemination costs.** Include them when required or scientifically justified.

## Gate 13: Commercialization And Market Evidence

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the first paying customer segment.** Identify the buyer, daily user, problem, trigger, and purchasing process.

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the business model hypothesis.** Compare practice subscription, usage, and network licensing.

- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the go-to-market sequence.** Cover discovery, prototype, controlled pilot, production pilot, and conversion.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define regulatory and reimbursement assumptions.** State whether revenue depends on reimbursement and what procurement barriers exist.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define Phase II commercialization milestones.** Include production readiness, pilot conversion, integration priorities, security, and sales evidence.
- [ ] **BLOCKER - Define the company's intellectual-property strategy.** Address copyright, trade secret, potential patents, trademarks, data rights, and freedom-to-operate review.

## Final Go/No-Go Review

- [ ] **GO - Opportunity:** The selected NOFO is active, eligible, mission-aligned, and financially workable.
- [ ] **GO - Registrations:** Every required registration is active and consistent.
- [ ] **GO - Research:** The application tests a real uncertainty with measurable aims and credible methods.
- [ ] **GO - Scope:** Phase I is achievable within the time, budget, team, and data available.
- [ ] **GO - Team:** The PI and key personnel are eligible, committed, and appropriately qualified.
- [ ] **GO - Partners:** Required advisors, sites, consultants, and subrecipients are documented.
- [ ] **GO - Ethics:** Human-subjects, privacy, consent, data, and regulatory pathways are correctly addressed.
- [ ] **GO - Security:** The prototype and research plan use appropriate safeguards and do not overclaim production readiness.
- [ ] **GO - Budget:** Every cost is allowable, reasonable, allocable, supported, and reconciled.
- [ ] **GO - Commercialization:** The buyer, market, differentiation, business model, and Phase II path are credible.
- [ ] **GO - Attachments:** Every required component is final, compliant, correctly placed, and within limits.
- [ ] **GO - Validation:** The complete electronic application passes portal checks and page-by-page review.
- [ ] **GO - Authorization:** The PI and AOR have approved the exact package being submitted.
- [ ] **GO - Contingency:** The team has documented award, resubmission, alternate-grant, and investor paths.
