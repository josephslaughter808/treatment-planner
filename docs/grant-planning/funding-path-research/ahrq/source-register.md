## Verification Rule

Before relying on any item:

1. Open the official opportunity page.
2. Confirm it is marked open or posted.
3. Confirm the expiration and next due date.
4. Confirm applicant eligibility.
5. Confirm that small businesses or for-profit organizations may lead.
6. Confirm the budget and project period.
7. Confirm ClearPath's proposed research is responsive.
8. Confirm details with program staff when the fit is uncertain.
