# AHRQ Funding Research

## Current Conclusion

AHRQ is an excellent scientific and health-services research fit for ClearPath, but no currently open, directly applicable AHRQ opportunity was identified during the June 20, 2026 review.

The opportunities and notices highlighted on AHRQ's Digital Healthcare Research funding page are stale or expired:

- `PA-24-261`, the AI healthcare-safety R18, expired March 17, 2026.
- `NOT-HS-24-004`, the disability special-emphasis notice, was active only through November 20, 2025.
- `NOT-HS-22-004`, the digital healthcare safety special-emphasis notice, points applicants to standing R18, R01, and R03 announcements that expired in 2024.

The reviewed AHRQ research-grant mechanisms also state that for-profit organizations cannot lead. A for-profit company may participate as a consortium member or subcontractor when the lead recipient has a substantive research role.

## Preliminary Decision

Do not replace the NLM SBIR strategy with AHRQ at this time.

Recommended actions:

1. Contact AHRQ Digital Healthcare Research staff to confirm whether a new R01, R18, R03, cooperative agreement, contract, or small-business-compatible opportunity is expected.
2. Ask whether a Dallas nonprofit or university could lead a future project with ClearPath as a substantive subcontractor.
3. Continue using AHRQ's digital safety, human factors, patient engagement, and implementation priorities to strengthen the NLM application.
4. Monitor Grants.gov, AHRQ funding announcements, and SAM.gov procurements.
