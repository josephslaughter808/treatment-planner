# Temporary Funding Path Research

## Purpose

This temporary workspace stores detailed research about each possible grant or federal funding path being considered for ClearPath.

Each funding option should eventually receive its own subfolder containing:

- Current funding status
- Scientific and commercial fit
- Eligible applicant types
- Available mechanisms
- Budget and project-period rules
- Application requirements
- Review criteria
- Award terms and reporting
- Contact questions
- Official source register
- ClearPath recommendation

| USDA DLT | Strong partner-led rural deployment route, not product R&D | FY2026 Distance Learning and Telemedicine Grant | $50,000 to $750,000; 15% nonfederal match; three-year period | **Not independently.** For-profits may apply, but the project needs qualifying rural sites, a telemedicine service network, an equipment/software deployment plan, and a match. | HRSA | Usually partner- and service-delivery-led | Several targeted FY2026 NOFOs | Varies by NOFO; one relevant example, RCORP-Impact, offers up to $750,000 per year for four years | **Not currently for the highlighted programs.** We would need the required rural or underserved population, service-delivery network, organizational experience, and program-specific partners. | 8 |

| Texas HHSC | Strong Texas partnership opportunity, not a direct company grant | `HHS0017228` through an eligible rural hospital or clinic | $1 million to $2 million from a $10 million program pool | **No as lead.** Eligible rural hospitals and rural health clinics must apply. | DoD | Topic-specific military need required | DSIP SBIR/STTR topic | Phase I: up to $323,090; Phase II: up to $2,153,927, subject to topic instructions | **Not yet.** We may meet basic small-business rules, but we need an exact open military-health topic, a defense customer, registrations, and contract-accounting readiness. | 11 |
| NIDILRR | Conditional on a disability-centered product and study | Annual NIDILRR SBIR Phase I competition | Phase I: up to $100,000 for about six months; Phase II: up to $575,000 for up to 24 months | **Not yet.** ClearPath may meet basic small-business rules, but it needs a defined disability population, accessible co-design, and outcomes in health/function, community living, or employment. No open FY2026 Phase I was confirmed. | 12 |
| ASTP/ONC | Excellent standards alignment, weak current NOFO fit | Future targeted cooperative agreement | Varies by NOFO | **No for the current LEAP areas.** ClearPath does not cleanly match the active research areas; future eligibility will depend on the specific NOFO and may require a consortium. | 13 |
| PCORI | Strong later-stage research fit, poor current product-development fit | Cycle 3 2026 Broad Pragmatic Studies | Up to $5 million in Category 1 and up to $12 million in Categories 2-3; up to five years | **Not research-ready.** For-profits are eligible, but the study must compare at least two established care strategies at clinical scale with patients as research partners. PCORI will not fund initial app development. | 14 |
| VA | Strong use-case relevance, poor direct grant access | VA partnership or procurement | VA intramural awards vary; current BRAVE awards offer up to $100,000 to eligible VA inventors | **No for direct intramural funding.** The founder is not a qualifying VA-employed investigator or VA inventor. ClearPath could participate through a VA-led partnership, subcontract, pilot, or procurement. | 15 |
| AHRQ | Excellent scientific fit, poor direct applicant fit | Future university/nonprofit-led project | Varies by mechanism; no current directly applicable award was identified | **No as a direct lead under the reviewed mechanisms.** A for-profit company would generally need to participate under an eligible university or nonprofit lead. | 16 |

This ranking is about the next practical application, not the long-term value of a relationship. AHRQ, ASTP/ONC, and VA can still be important validation, pilot, standards, or implementation partners.

Award amounts are planning estimates based on the opportunities reviewed on June 20, 2026. They are ceilings or examples, not promised award amounts. The live NOFO, institute participation rules, and agency confirmation control.

## Status Warning

Funding announcements change. Every opportunity must be rechecked against its official announcement immediately before making an application decision.

This folder is temporary research material. Final selections and approved application strategy should be moved into the main grant-planning documents after program-staff confirmation.
