# Texas HHSC Rural Pediatric Tele-Connectivity Deep Dive

## Review Date

June 20, 2026

## Executive Summary

Texas HHSC's `HHS0017228` funds pediatric tele-connectivity for rural Texas. It is a substantial active program, but eligible rural hospitals and rural health clinics must lead. ClearPath is a possible vendor or technology partner, not the direct applicant.

The opportunity should be used to start relationships with rural providers, not to force the company into a hurried pediatric-only strategy.

## Current Opportunity

- Procurement number: `HHS0017228`
- Release date: June 3, 2026
- Submission deadline: July 13, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. Central
- Total announced program pool: up to $10 million
- Expected individual awards: $1 million to $2 million
- Eligible leads: qualifying Texas rural hospitals and rural health clinics under the RFA

The program connects eligible rural sites with pediatric specialists, pediatric subspecialists, or qualifying higher-education members of the Texas Child Mental Health Care Consortium.

## Purpose And Allowable Direction

The state announcement describes funding for pediatric telemedicine access. Potentially funded categories include:

- Information-technology infrastructure
- Security
- Services and supplies needed for telemedicine
- Secure two-way video and related equipment
- Connections between rural clinical sites and remote specialists

The RFA defines allowable costs, procurement, reporting, project dates, and applicant qualifications. ClearPath should not assume that software-development labor, ownership of funded IP, subscriptions, or a sole-source vendor arrangement are allowable.

## ClearPath's Possible Role

ClearPath could support:

- Patient or guardian authorization for pediatric record exchange
- Prior-record collection before a remote specialty visit
- Transfer of consent-scoped clinical packages
- Request status and audit history
- Coordination among rural site, specialist, parent/guardian, and originating office
- Human-readable and standards-based exchange formats

The role must solve a real pediatric telemedicine workflow identified by the applicant. It cannot be a generic product demonstration attached to a telemedicine grant.

## Eligibility Reality

ClearPath does not currently meet lead-applicant requirements. A qualifying rural hospital or clinic must control the application and program.

Participation may require:

- Competitive vendor selection or other approved procurement
- A subcontract or service agreement
- Defined deliverables and pricing
- Texas vendor registration
- Insurance and contracting requirements
- Security, privacy, accessibility, and data-governance review
- Background IP and funded-development terms

The hospital's procurement and legal teams must determine the allowable structure.

## Timing And Readiness

The July 13 deadline leaves limited time. ClearPath does not yet have a production-grade PHI environment, completed penetration testing, or a proven pediatric telemedicine deployment.

The responsible near-term action is to learn which rural organizations are applying and whether they have a future need. It would be risky to promise live PHI handling or a finished integration on the current timeline.

## Pediatric And Guardian Complexity

A genuine future project would need research and legal work on:

- Parent and guardian authority
- Adolescent confidentiality
- Minor consent rights
- Sensitive service categories
- Custody and delegated authority
- Emergency care
- Revocation and expiration
- Accessibility and language
- Interstate specialist services where applicable

These issues increase the value of ClearPath's authorization model but also increase legal and product risk.

## Contact Questions

1. Which rural hospitals and clinics are eligible and considering an application?
2. May software vendors be included, and under what procurement rules?
3. Are product-development costs allowable or only deployment costs?
4. What project period and implementation deadline apply?
5. What security and PHI requirements must be met before deployment?
6. Who owns software improvements funded through the grant?

## Recommendation

Contact Texas rural-health associations and eligible providers for discovery, while being explicit that ClearPath is pre-pilot. Use the opportunity to understand procurement and workflow needs. Do not present ClearPath as the direct applicant or promise July-ready production deployment.
