# Texas HHSC Funding Research

## Current Conclusion

Texas `HHS0017228` provides $1 million to $2 million awards for pediatric tele-connectivity through eligible rural hospitals and rural health clinics. ClearPath cannot lead but could participate as a selected technology partner or vendor.

## Preliminary Decision

Treat the July 13, 2026 opportunity as partner discovery, not a rushed direct application. Contact eligible applicants only with a clear, compliant role and no claim that ClearPath itself is eligible.
