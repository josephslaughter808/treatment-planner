# NSF Seed Fund Research

## Current Conclusion

America's Seed Fund powered by NSF is ClearPath's strongest non-NIH Phase I option. The active consolidated solicitation is `NSF 26-510`. Phase I provides up to $305,000 for 6 to 18 months, and the current full-proposal deadlines include July 27, 2026; November 4, 2026; and March 4, 2027.

NSF funds high-risk technological innovation with commercial potential. It does not fund routine engineering, incremental product features, or a service business whose main risk is execution.

## Preliminary Decision

Prepare and submit a Project Pitch after defining the core technical risk. Keep NSF as a parallel option to NLM, but do not submit the same work to both agencies simultaneously without disclosing overlap and structuring clearly distinct proposals.
