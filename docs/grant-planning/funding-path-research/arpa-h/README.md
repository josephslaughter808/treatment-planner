# ARPA-H Funding Research

## Current Conclusion

ARPA-H can support unusually ambitious health technology, but its current 2026 SBIR/STTR topics do not match ClearPath. Its broader Mission Office Innovative Solutions Openings may accept a concept, yet they demand a transformational program with aggressive milestones and national-scale impact.

## Documents

- [ARPA-H Deep Dive](./arpa-h-deep-dive.md)
- [ARPA-H Source Register](./source-register.md)

## Preliminary Decision

Do not force ClearPath into the current ARPA-H SBIR solicitation. Monitor future topics and consider a short Solution Summary to the Resilient Systems or Scalable Solutions mission office only after the technical thesis and measurable national impact are much stronger.
