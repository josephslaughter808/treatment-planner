# VA Official Source Register

Reviewed June 20, 2026.

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [VA Health Systems Research funding](https://www.hsrd.research.va.gov/funding/) | Intramural research mechanisms and guidance |
| [QUERI funding opportunities](https://www.queri.research.va.gov/funding/opportunities.cfm) | VA-employee PI requirement and partnership route |
| [VA research eligibility](https://www.research.va.gov/resources/policies/ProgramGuide-1200-15-Eligibility-for-VA-Research-Support.pdf) | PI and appointment requirements |
| [BRAVE funding program](https://www.research.va.gov/programs/tech_transfer/brave.cfm) | Current VA-owned technology commercialization program |
| [VA Technology Transfer](https://www.research.va.gov/programs/tech_transfer/) | External collaboration and IP context |
| [Doing business with VA](https://www.va.gov/OSDBU/) | Small-business and procurement entry point |
| [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov/) | Federal registration and contract opportunities |

VA staff and the local research or contracting office must confirm the correct pathway before sharing sensitive information or proposing integration.
