# VA Funding Research

## Current Conclusion

The Department of Veterans Affairs is a compelling future customer and pilot environment for cross-system record exchange, especially where Veterans receive both VA and community care. It is not a practical direct Phase I grant source for ClearPath today.

VA's major health-services research and implementation programs are intramural. Principal investigators generally must be VA employees, and many opportunities are available only through VA systems. External companies more often enter through a VA investigator partnership, procurement, subcontract, cooperative research relationship, or innovation challenge.

## Preliminary Decision

Treat VA as a partnership and acquisition pathway. Identify a Dallas-area VA clinical or research champion, but finance initial R&D through NLM or NSF.
