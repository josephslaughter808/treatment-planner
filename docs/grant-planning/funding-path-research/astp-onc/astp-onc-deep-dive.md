# ASTP/ONC Deep Dive

## Review Date

June 20, 2026

## Executive Summary

The Assistant Secretary for Technology Policy and Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology shapes the national interoperability environment in which ClearPath must operate. Its work on certified health IT, USCDI, FHIR, patient access, information exchange, and nationwide interoperability is strategically important.

ASTP/ONC does not maintain a predictable startup Phase I program. It releases targeted grants and cooperative agreements for specific policy, standards, and research needs.

## Current Opportunity: LEAP In Health IT 2026

1. Standards-based agentic artificial intelligence in clinical care
2. A secure community feedback layer supporting the Lantern FHIR endpoint-monitoring ecosystem

ClearPath does not directly fit any area. ## Why ASTP/ONC Matters To ClearPath

ClearPath's product strategy should remain aligned with:

- FHIR APIs and implementation guides
- USCDI data classes and elements
- TEFCA and Qualified Health Information Network developments
- Certified health-IT requirements
- Patient access and information-blocking rules
- Provenance, identity, consent, security, and audit standards
- Burden reduction and usable clinical workflows

ASTP alignment improves technical credibility even when ASTP is not the funder.

## Funding Mechanics

ASTP/ONC opportunities are normally announced individually and submitted through Grants.gov. Each NOFO controls:

- Eligible organization types
- Award count and ceiling
- Project period
- Cost sharing
- Required partnerships
- Page limits and attachments
- Review criteria
- Reporting and data deliverables

Applicants should not infer rules from an older LEAP round.

## Cooperative Agreement Implications

Many ASTP awards are cooperative agreements, meaning substantial federal involvement. Awardees may be expected to coordinate with ASTP staff, standards communities, federal partners, and other recipients; share findings; produce reusable artifacts; and obtain approval for material changes.

Standard HHS and award-specific terms may include:

- Financial and performance reporting
- Prior approval for scope, key-personnel, carryover, extension, or major rebudgeting changes
- Final reports after the project ends
- Federal rights to use funded software, data, or copyrighted works under the award terms
- Human-subjects, privacy, security, accessibility, and records obligations

Any software and data-rights language must be reviewed by counsel before submission.
