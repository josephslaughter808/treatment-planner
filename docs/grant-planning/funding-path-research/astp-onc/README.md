# ASTP/ONC Funding Research

## Current Conclusion

ASTP/ONC is one of ClearPath's best policy and standards fits, but it is not a standing startup grant program. Its current `LEAP in Health IT 2026` opportunity is narrowly focused on agentic AI standards, the Lantern FHIR endpoint ecosystem, and laboratory data quality. ClearPath does not cleanly match those areas.

## Documents

- [ASTP/ONC Deep Dive](./astp-onc-deep-dive.md)
- [ASTP/ONC Source Register](./source-register.md)

## Preliminary Decision

Use ASTP/ONC as a standards compass and future targeted-funding monitor. Do not apply to the current LEAP opportunity unless the project is genuinely redesigned around a listed research area and partner expertise.
