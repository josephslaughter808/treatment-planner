# DoD Funding Research

## Current Conclusion

The Department of Defense SBIR/STTR program can provide substantial non-dilutive R&D contracts, but applications must answer a published military topic. The current program lists Phase I awards up to $323,090, typically 3 to 12 months, and Phase II up to $2,153,927, typically 15 to 24 months, subject to topic-specific instructions.

No current Defense Health Agency topic clearly matching ClearPath was confirmed in this review. General healthcare usefulness is not enough; the proposal needs a military-health customer, operational need, and exact open topic.

## Preliminary Decision

Monitor DSIP for Defense Health Agency and service medical topics. Pursue DoD only when a topic explicitly calls for health-information exchange, care transitions, readiness, distributed care, or patient-mediated interoperability.
