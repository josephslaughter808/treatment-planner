# PCORI Funding Research

## Current Conclusion

PCORI can fund for-profit research organizations and healthcare-delivery comparisons at substantial scale. It is not appropriate for ClearPath's initial build. It becomes relevant after the platform and comparator workflows are established and clinical partners can conduct rigorous patient-centered comparative-effectiveness research.

## Documents

- [PCORI Deep Dive](./pcori-deep-dive.md)
- [PCORI Source Register](./source-register.md)

## Preliminary Decision

Treat PCORI as a Phase II or post-pilot evidence strategy. Begin learning its patient-partnership requirements now, but do not prepare a Cycle 3 application without mature interventions and research partners.
