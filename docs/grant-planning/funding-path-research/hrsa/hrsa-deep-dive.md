# HRSA Deep Dive

## Review Date

June 20, 2026

## Executive Summary

The Health Resources and Services Administration funds access to healthcare for underserved populations, health workforce development, rural health, health centers, maternal and child health, HIV care, organ systems, and related service infrastructure.

HRSA is a program-delivery funder more than a general software R&D funder. ClearPath can participate when technology enables a specific funded healthcare service, population, network, and measurable access outcome.

## Current FY2026 Landscape

HRSA's live funding list changes frequently. During this review, relevant or adjacent open items included:

### Technology-Enabled Learning Collaborative Program: `HRSA-26-053`

- Application period shown: June 5 to July 8, 2026
- Eligible applicants include domestic public or private nonprofit or for-profit entities
- Applicants must have demonstrated, established experience using telehealth technologies to serve underserved populations

This is not a clean ClearPath R&D fit. ClearPath currently lacks the documented telehealth-service history implied by the eligibility language.

### Telehealth Center Of Excellence: `HRSA-26-056`

- Application period shown: June 5 to July 8, 2026
- Supports academic medical centers implementing and evaluating telehealth solutions in rural and medically underserved communities
- Current eligibility is limited to public academic medical centers that are incumbent Telehealth Center of Excellence recipients

ClearPath cannot lead. It could only participate if an eligible incumbent center selected it as a partner or vendor.

### Rural Communities Opioid Response Program Impact: `HRSA-26-037`

- Deadline shown: July 8, 2026
- Project period: four years
- Up to $750,000 per year as described by HRSA
- Domestic public, private, nonprofit, and for-profit entities may apply under the current summary
- Requires a rural service area and a serious substance-use-disorder treatment and recovery network

This is financially significant but not a product-development opportunity. ClearPath would need to become part of a real rural SUD service-delivery program with qualified clinical, community, workforce, and evaluation partners.

### Telehealth Nutrition Services Network: `HRSA-26-076`

- Deadline shown: July 8, 2026
- Focuses on nutrition services in rural and underserved primary and specialty care

This is too program-specific unless ClearPath is supporting a qualified nutrition-care network.

## Dallas And Rural Eligibility

Living or incorporating in Dallas does not disqualify ClearPath from HRSA. Many HRSA programs allow an urban applicant when services reach eligible rural or underserved communities. However, each NOFO defines geography, eligible service areas, and required evidence.

ClearPath should not claim rural eligibility based on the founder's address. It must identify qualifying service sites and verify them with HRSA's rural eligibility tools and the applicable NOFO.

Potential Texas partners include:

- Rural hospitals and clinics
- Federally qualified health centers
- Academic medical centers
- Regional health partnerships
- Behavioral-health and substance-use networks
- Telehealth programs
- Public-health entities

Partner selection should follow the research and service need, not be reverse-engineered solely around an open grant.

## Application And Award Rules

Most HRSA applications require:

- SAM.gov/UEI and Grants.gov registrations
- HRSA Electronic Handbooks access after award and sometimes during process steps
- A detailed project narrative and work plan
- Organizational capability and partner documentation
- Budget and budget narrative
- Performance measures and evaluation
- Sustainability and dissemination plans
- Attachments and page limits defined by the NOFO

Awards may be grants or cooperative agreements. Cost sharing, indirect costs, salary limits, project period, and allowable technology expenses are opportunity-specific. A negotiated indirect-cost rate agreement may be required to claim a full rate; de minimis treatment depends on current federal and HHS rules.

## Founder Salary And Developer Costs

Reasonable salary and technical costs may be allowable when they directly support approved program activities and are adequately documented. HRSA will not fund an $80,000 founder salary merely because the founder needs to leave a job. The application must tie effort, duties, rate, and outcomes to the funded program.

General startup development, investor work, sales, unrelated features, and commercial operations are not chargeable to an award.

## ClearPath Fit Models

### Weak Model

ClearPath applies alone to finish the app and later find users.

### Better Model

An eligible healthcare network identifies delayed records as a barrier to continuity; ClearPath supports patient-authorized exchange; the project measures access, timeliness, completion, safety, and equity in a defined population.

### Strongest Model

The network, implementation team, clinical leadership, community representation, data governance, and evaluator are committed before submission, and the technology is one component of a credible service intervention.

## Contact Questions

1. Can a small for-profit lead the specific NOFO?
2. Must the applicant have prior service-delivery or telehealth experience?
3. Can software development be included, and what portion is reasonable?
4. Which Texas communities and facilities meet the geography rules?
5. Is an academic, health-system, or nonprofit lead preferred?
6. What indirect-cost documentation and salary limitations apply?

## Recommendation

Monitor HRSA and begin partner discovery, but do not rush into a July 2026 application without an established program network. HRSA is a plausible Phase II demonstration or implementation route after ClearPath has feasibility evidence and service partners.
