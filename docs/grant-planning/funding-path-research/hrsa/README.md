# HRSA Funding Research

## Current Conclusion

HRSA funds healthcare access, workforce, rural health, safety-net services, telehealth, maternal and child health, and other delivery programs. It can fund technology as part of a defined service-delivery intervention, but it is generally not a general-purpose software R&D funder.

Several FY2026 opportunities are live, including telehealth and rural-health programs. Their eligibility and program aims vary. ClearPath would need an eligible delivery network, demonstrated experience, a specific population, and measurable access outcomes.

## Preliminary Decision

Do not replace the NLM/NSF product-research strategy with HRSA. Explore HRSA through a rural or underserved-care partner, with ClearPath functioning as the enabling platform inside a service-delivery study.
