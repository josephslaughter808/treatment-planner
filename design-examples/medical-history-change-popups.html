Provider check-in alert concepts

Make patient changes impossible to miss.

These are six possible directions for showing new medical history changes after a patient scans or enters their ClearPath code. The goal is clear, urgent, and professional without feeling scary or chaotic.

Design goal

Red outline. Clear list. Required acknowledgement.

My vote is option 1 or option 4 for the first production version. Option 1 is the safest and most direct. Option 4 gives the office the clearest before/after comparison.

Option 1

Centered Critical Popup

Classic modal. The office cannot miss it and must acknowledge changes before continuing.
