Theme 3 Layout Lab

Same visual family. Six completely different layout systems.

These all keep the `Modern Evidence` tone, but they stop relying on repeated rounded info bubbles. The goal here is to choose how the product should actually organize information and move the eye, not just how it should be colored.

A left utility rail with a more open, magazine-like treatment canvas. Feels premium, modern, and composed without looking like a dashboard.
